Frequently Asked Questions

Did Greystar settle with the FTC?

Yes. On Dec. 2, 2025, Greystar agreed to pay $24 million to resolve the hidden-fee lawsuit brought by the FTC and Colorado: $23 million to the FTC for consumer refunds and $1 million to Colorado. It also agreed to show total monthly prices, including mandatory fees, in its rental listings. The case had been filed in January 2025.

Sources: 1, 2

How much has Greystar paid in settlements?

Monetary settlements in this archive include $50 million in the RealPage renters' class action (proposed, Oct. 2025); $24 million to the FTC and Colorado (Dec. 2025); $7 million to nine state attorneys general (Nov. 2025); $4.665 million in a North Carolina eviction-fee class action (2022); $2.5 million in a Washington screening-fee class action (2022); about $1.43 million over servicemember lease charges (2025); $100,000 in Arizona restitution (2026); $25,000 in an EEOC case (2014); and $10,000 to a California complainant (2026). Several settlements state that Greystar admitted no wrongdoing.

Sources: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9

What is the Greystar RealPage case about?

Government and private lawsuits allege that landlords, including Greystar, shared nonpublic data through RealPage's rent-pricing software to align rents. The Justice Department added Greystar to its suit in January 2025 and reached a proposed settlement in August 2025 barring Greystar from using pricing software that relies on rivals' nonpublic data; Greystar said it admitted no wrongdoing. Greystar also agreed to pay $50 million in the renters' class action and $7 million to nine states. Suits by Arizona, D.C., New Jersey and Washington remain pending as to Greystar in our records.

Sources: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8

Who is Bob Faith?

Bob Faith is the founder and CEO of Greystar. In April 2020 he was named to the real estate panel of President Trump's Great American Economic Revival Industry Groups. He was also named personally as a defendant in a 2023 self-represented tenant lawsuit; the case was closed that year.

Sources: 1, 2, 3

Is Greystar accused of Section 8 voucher discrimination?

Yes, as allegations. In July 2026 the Housing Rights Initiative filed 114 complaints with agencies in California, Hawaii, Maryland, Michigan, New Jersey, Virginia and D.C., alleging, based on undercover testing, that Greystar properties rejected or set unlawful conditions for Housing Choice Voucher holders. The complaints are pending. In 2022 a Greystar entity signed a five-year fair housing agreement covering its Virginia properties after voucher testing by the Equal Rights Center.

Sources: 1, 2

What did Greystar pay over military servicemember lease fees?

In June 2025 the Justice Department said Greystar Management Services would set aside $1.35 million for servicemembers and co-tenants charged early-termination fees after relocation orders, and pay a $77,370 civil penalty, resolving allegations under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act.

Sources: 1

What was the $860 million Greystar verdict?

In April 2023 a Dallas County jury awarded about $860 million to the parents of a resident killed when a tower crane at a Greystar development fell onto the Elan City Lights apartments in a June 2019 storm. Post-trial status is not confirmed in our records.

Sources: 1, 2
