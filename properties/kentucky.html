Reporting

Students react to The Province ownership change

The University of Louisville student paper reported unsanitary conditions, inconsistent communication and a broken rent-payment portal as The Province transitioned from American Campus Communities to Greystar. It also noted WDRB reporting on student complaints of broken air conditioning and unsanitary conditions at Greystar-managed Trifecta Apartments.

published

Regulators

Kentucky AG sues RealPage and landlords including Greystar; Greystar later settles

Kentucky AG Russell Coleman sued RealPage and several landlords, including Greystar Real Estate Partners, alleging algorithmic price-fixing in violation of federal antitrust law and the Kentucky Consumer Protection Act. According to PYMNTS (July 21, 2026), Kentucky reached a settlement with Greystar Management Services; terms were not disclosed in public filings.

Greystar settled July 2026 (terms undisclosed)
Jul 2, 2025 · Kentucky Attorney General / U.S. District Court, E.D. Ky. · Kentucky

Regulators

NLRB issues complaint against Greystar Management Services over coercive work rules

After an unfair labor practice charge filed in January 2017, the NLRB regional office issued a complaint and notice of hearing alleging violations of Section 8(a)(1), including coercive rules and retaliation for concerted activity. A Regional Director order was issued in June 2018 and the case is closed.

closed (2018)
Feb 23, 2017 · National Labor Relations Board (Case 09-CA-190927) · Louisville, KY
