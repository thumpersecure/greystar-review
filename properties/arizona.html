Greystar settlement will result in refunds for some Arizona renters

The Copper Courier reported that Arizona renters could recoup money paid to Greystar for hidden and junk fees from $1.6 million in settlements involving two Phoenix-area complexes.

Regulators

Arizona AG announces three settlements with Greystar over hidden rental charges and junk fees

Arizona AG Kris Mayes announced three Assurances of Discontinuance entered in Maricopa County Superior Court: one adopting the injunctive terms of the FTC/Colorado settlement (with about $1.5M in anticipated restitution to Arizona consumers from the FTC fund), and two resolving alleged Arizona Consumer Fraud Act junk-fee violations at The Julia and Avana Gilbert with a combined $100,000 in restitution.

settled (assurances of discontinuance)
May 13, 2026 · Arizona Attorney General · The Julia; Avana Gilbert · Phoenix, AZ

Regulators

Arizona AG sues RealPage and nine landlords, including Greystar, over alleged rent price-fixing

Arizona alleged RealPage and nine landlords, including Greystar Management Services, L.P., conspired to raise rents in the Phoenix and Tucson metro areas by pooling competitively sensitive pricing and occupancy data in RealPage's software.

pending
Feb 28, 2024 · Arizona Attorney General · Phoenix, AZ
