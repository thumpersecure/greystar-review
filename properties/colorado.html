Regulators

Colorado settles hidden fees lawsuit against Greystar in the latest case resolved with landlord giant

Denver Post coverage of the FTC/Colorado $24 million hidden-fees settlement (ftc-colorado-greystar-24m-settlement-2025). It adds that the suit described two Denver complexes where fees allegedly pushed costs above advertised rent, and that Greystar operates more than 45,000 units in Colorado. It also quotes Greystar saying the deal 'enhances transparency' and notes Colorado's new rent-fee law takes effect Jan. 1.

settled $24M
Dec 2, 2025 · The Denver Post · Colorado

Regulators

Attorney General Phil Weiser, FTC announce $24M settlement with Greystar for misleading renters about costs, saddling tenants with hidden fees

Colorado's announcement of the FTC/Colorado hidden-fees settlement already recorded from the FTC side (ftc-colorado-greystar-24m-settlement-2025). This source adds the split: Colorado receives $1 million for costs, enforcement and consumer education, while the FTC receives $23 million for nationwide relief. It also lists the disclosure terms: total monthly price shown upfront, every fee itemized as mandatory or optional, and no upfront payment before full pricing is disclosed.

settled $24M ($23M FTC, $1M Colorado)
Dec 2, 2025 · Colorado Attorney General (coag.gov) · Colorado

Colorado reaches settlement with landlord giant Greystar in price-fixing lawsuit

Polis vetoed a 2025 bill banning RealPage-style software. It also reports Greystar's statement that it was 'pleased this matter is resolved.'

settled $7M (proposed consent decree, pending before the court)

Regulators

Attorney General Phil Weiser announces $7M settlement with Greystar for price-fixing scheme that drove up rents for Coloradans

Colorado AG release on the nine-state $7 million RealPage settlement (nine-state-ag-greystar-7m-settlement-2025). It adds that Colorado receives more than $1 million for antitrust and consumer-protection work. It also says Greystar is barred from revenue-management software that uses competitors' nonpublic data, and that the settling states may share in Greystar's cooperation with DOJ.

Court Docket

Littleton tenant sues Greystar alleging eviction in retaliation for silica dust complaints

A former tenant sued Greystar Management Services and a concrete contractor, alleging he was exposed to silica dust from concrete work, reported it to Greystar and OSHA (which fined the contractor more than $58,000), and was then evicted for allegedly 'photographing the vendors without consent.' Greystar denies most allegations and says it had no control over the contractor's work.

pending (as reported)
Jul 29, 2025 · Denver7 (KMGH) · Mountain Gate Apartments · Littleton, CO

Reporting

Littleton apartment tenants describe persistent bird infestation 'nightmare'

Tenants said hundreds of swallows nest on Building 8 each spring, leaving droppings, dead birds and bird mites that they say enter apartments, and described unfulfilled promises from management. Greystar told 9NEWS its options are limited because the swallows are protected under the Migratory Bird Treaty Act and it is deterring nesting in compliance with federal law.

published
Jun 2025 · 9NEWS (KUSA) · Mountain Gate · Littleton, CO

Welton Park Apartments Go More Than a Week Without Water

Yellow Scene reported that residents of Welton Park, managed by Greystar, went about ten days without clean running water affecting roughly 195 units, and described the outage as part of a pattern of alleged mismanagement.

published

Reporting

Colorado family charged $4,000 in fees after loved one dies unexpectedly, landlord says death meant she broke her lease

After tenant Leticia Farrer died in January 2024, her family was billed about $4,140 in early-termination and concession fees at the Greystar-managed Avenida; the lease said tenants were not released 'for any reason, including death.' Greystar cleared the balance after the story; Colorado later passed 'Letty's Act' (HB25-1108) to bar such fees.

balance waived after coverage
Jan 29, 2024 · CBS Colorado (KCNC) · Avenida · Loveland, CO
