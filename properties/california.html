Greystar Apartments in California

The Greystar Review lists 596 apartment communities in California that appear in Greystar's public property directory, across 151 cities including Los Angeles, San Diego and San Francisco. This archive also contains 20 lawsuits, regulator actions, news reports or tenant complaints connected to California, listed below. Listing a property here only means it is Greystar-managed or -owned per that directory; it does not mean any complaint has been made about it.

Records from California

Regulators

Housing watchdog files 114 voucher-discrimination complaints against Greystar with seven state and D.C. agencies

Housing Rights Initiative filed complaints with the Virginia OAG, California CRD, Maryland Commission on Civil Rights, Hawai'i Civil Rights Commission, Michigan DCR, New Jersey DCR and D.C. Office of Human Rights alleging 114 violations of source-of-income laws. The complaints allege, based on undercover testing, that Greystar properties rejected Housing Choice Voucher holders, imposed unlawful conditions, and refused to count voucher assistance toward income requirements.

complaints pending (allegations)
Jul 14, 2026 · Cohen Milstein (counsel) / Housing Rights Initiative · CA, HI, MD, MI, NJ, VA, DC

Regulators

Greystar California agrees to revise tenant screening statewide after fair housing complaint

CRD announced a settlement with Greystar California, Inc. after a complaint alleging an applicant at a Hollywood apartment complex was denied based on criminal history without an opportunity to explain. Greystar agreed to review and revise screening policies statewide, notify staff of California's criminal-history protections, train specified Southern California staff, and pay the complainant $10,000.

settled $10,000 plus policy changes
Jan 13, 2026 · California Civil Rights Department · Los Angeles, CA

Greystar reaches $7M settlement in antitrust lawsuit over rent increases

Bay Area coverage of the nine-state $7 million RealPage settlement announced by California AG Rob Bonta (nine-state-ag-greystar-7m-settlement-2025). It adds a Greystar spokesperson statement and lists Greystar-managed properties in Mountain View and on the Peninsula. Bonta's office said it could not say whether Santa Clara or San Mateo County properties were affected.

settled $7M (multistate)
Nov 28, 2025 · Pleasanton Weekly · Mountain View, CA
