Court Docket

Greystar, others settle RealPage rental pricing lawsuit

Trade-press coverage of the RealPage MDL class settlements in the Middle District of Tennessee (realpage-mdl-greystar-50m-class-settlement-2025). It adds the full picture: 26 class settlements worth $141.8 million, with Greystar's $50 million the largest and Simpson, Avenue5, Bell, Bozzuto, Pinnacle and Winn also settling. Settlers agreed to stop sharing nonpublic data with RealPage's rent-setting algorithm, with no admission of wrongdoing.

settled $50M (class settlement pending before the court)
Oct 8, 2025 · Multifamily Dive · Nashville, TN

Court Docket

Greystar agrees to $50 million settlement in RealPage rental pricing lawsuit

Reuters report on Greystar's $50 million settlement of the renter class action in the RealPage MDL (realpage-mdl-greystar-50m-class-settlement-2025). Renters alleged Greystar and other landlords shared nonpublic data with RealPage to push rents above competitive levels. Greystar's deal was the largest in a group of preliminary class settlements totaling about $141 million.

settled $50M (class settlement pending before the court)
Oct 2, 2025 · Reuters · Nashville, TN

Court Docket

Greystar agrees to $50 million settlement in renters' RealPage price-fixing class action

In the consolidated private antitrust MDL alleging landlords used RealPage software to align rents, Greystar agreed to pay $50 million as part of more than $141 million in proposed settlements filed with 25+ landlords, and to stop sharing nonpublic data with RealPage. Greystar announced an agreement in principle on Aug. 8, 2025, stating it admitted no wrongdoing.

settled $50M (proposed class settlement)
Oct 1, 2025 · U.S. District Court, M.D. Tenn. (In re RealPage, Inc., Rental Software Antitrust Litigation (No. II), 3:23-md-03071) · Nashville, TN

Bellevue renter seeks assistance for growing mold problem

A tenant at Avana Lexington told NewsChannel 5 he no longer felt comfortable living in his apartment because of a spreading mold problem. A Greystar spokesperson said the resident had filed a work order two days earlier and the company was investigating.

published
Jul 11, 2023 · NewsChannel 5 (WTVF) · Avana Lexington · Nashville (Bellevue), TN

Regulators

HUD conciliation agreement with Greystar over alleged harassment of Hispanic tenants in Nashville

A HUD Secretary-initiated Fair Housing Act complaint filed Feb. 10, 2012 alleged TriTex Real Estate Advisors and Greystar Management Services subjected Hispanic residents of the 233-unit Clairmont Apartments to different terms, intimidation and harassment based on national origin. Respondents denied discrimination and entered a conciliation agreement without any finding of liability.

conciliation agreement
2013 · U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (FHEO Case No. 04-12-0360-8) · Clairmont Apartments · Nashville, TN
