Judge OKs Greystar Deal In DOJ's RealPage Price-Fixing Suit

According to Law360, a North Carolina federal judge gave final sign-off on March 2, 2026 to the Justice Department's antitrust settlement with Greystar Management Services in the federal RealPage rent price-fixing case. Regulators

North Carolina settles with Greystar in AI rent-fixing lawsuit

North Carolina coverage of the nine-state $7 million RealPage settlement announced by AG Jeff Jackson (nine-state-ag-greystar-7m-settlement-2025). It adds Jackson's estimate that more than 25,000 NC renters are affected and notes Greystar is the state's largest landlord. Third-party pricing software is allowed only under a court-appointed monitor, and Greystar and the states jointly moved to dismiss it from the 2024 federal suit.

settled $7M (multistate)
Nov 20, 2025 · NC Newsline · North Carolina

Court Docket

Greystar pays $4.665 million to settle North Carolina eviction-fee class action

Tenants alleged Greystar unlawfully charged North Carolina residents filing fees, sheriff service fees and attorneys' fees in eviction actions and threatened such fees in collection letters (2014-2018), in violation of state rental and debt-collection law. Greystar agreed to a $4,665,000 class settlement without admitting wrongdoing (final fairness hearing July 22, 2022).

settled $4.665M
Jul 22, 2022 · U.S. District Court, M.D.N.C. (Wallace v. Greystar Real Estate Partners, LLC, 1:18-cv-00501) · North Carolina
