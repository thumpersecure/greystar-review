Regulators

Housing watchdog files 114 voucher-discrimination complaints against Greystar with seven state and D.C. agencies

Housing Rights Initiative filed complaints with the Virginia OAG, California CRD, Maryland Commission on Civil Rights, Hawai'i Civil Rights Commission, Michigan DCR, New Jersey DCR and D.C. Office of Human Rights alleging 114 violations of source-of-income laws. The complaints allege, based on undercover testing, that Greystar properties rejected Housing Choice Voucher holders, imposed unlawful conditions, and refused to count voucher assistance toward income requirements.

complaints pending (allegations)
Jul 14, 2026 · Cohen Milstein (counsel) / Housing Rights Initiative · CA, HI, MD, MI, NJ, VA, DC

Court Docket

Hall v. Greystar Management Services: Fair Housing Act retaliation and eviction-property suit

A former tenant sued Greystar Management Services, a landscaping contractor and a sheriff's lieutenant, alleging Fair Housing Act retaliation, conversion and constitutional claims arising from the alleged deprivation of her personal property during a December 2011 eviction. The case went to the Fourth Circuit (No. 14-2145) and back; the district court issued further rulings in 2016 and the docket closed in February 2017 without a judgment for the plaintiff.

closed 2017 (plaintiff did not prevail)
Apr 12, 2016 · U.S. District Court, D. Md. (No. 1:13-cv-03615), 179 F. Supp. 3d 534 · Baltimore County, MD

Regulators

Greystar Management Services pays $25,000 to settle EEOC pregnancy discrimination suit

The EEOC sued in October 2011 alleging Greystar fired a housekeeper at a Rockville, Md., apartment complex after she disclosed her pregnancy and demanded medical documentation clearing her to work with cleaning chemicals. A 30-month consent decree required $25,000 in relief, an anti-discrimination policy, manager training and a posted notice.

settled $25,000 (consent decree)
Jun 17, 2014 · U.S. EEOC / U.S. District Court, D. Md. (No. 11-cv-02789) · Rockville, MD
