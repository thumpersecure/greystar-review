Records from Washington

Regulators

Greystar notifies Washington AG of data breach involving employee email account

Greystar Real Estate Partners filed a breach notice stating an unauthorized party accessed an employee email account between May 1 and May 18, 2026 and acquired files containing personal information; affected people were offered 24 months of credit monitoring. This is a mandatory disclosure, not an enforcement action; no lawsuit over this breach was found.

breach notice filed
Aug 27, 2026 · Washington State Attorney General (data breach notification filing) · Washington

Regulators

Washington AG sues RealPage and landlords including Greystar over alleged rent price-fixing

Filed in King County Superior Court, the suit names Greystar Management Services, LLC and GREP Washington, LLC among landlords alleged to have conspired with RealPage to raise and stabilize rents and exchange nonpublic competitive information in Washington.

pending
Apr 3, 2025 · Washington State Attorney General · Seattle, WA

Court Docket

Washington class action challenges Greystar lease buy-out and reletting charges

Filed on behalf of tenants who moved out early and were charged, or had deposits applied to, buy-out fees, reletting charges and/or future rent. Greystar removed the case to federal court, which remanded it to King County Superior Court.

pending (trial set Feb 2027 per counsel)
Apr 2025 · King County Superior Court (per plaintiffs' counsel Schroeter Goldmark & Bender) · Seattle, WA

Student alleges $2,005 pre-lease payment not refunded

Reviewer, a college student, alleges they were required to pay a month's rent before receiving any lease terms on a promise of a full refund if unsatisfied, and that requests for that refund went unanswered.

Tenant alleges failed toilet repair led to mold and deposit was kept

Reviewer alleges a toilet that broke again after repair was never fixed, leading to mold discovered at move-out, and that the full deposit was withheld although the damage stemmed from the repairs. Reviewer also reports unexplained rent increases.

published

Court Docket · The Chairman

Gamez v. Greystar: pro se civil rights suit naming Greystar and CEO Bob Faith

A self-represented tenant filed a civil rights suit against Greystar Real Estate Partners, Bob Faith (individually and in his official capacity), a property-owning LLC, Spokane County, a law firm and others. The district case closed in May 2023; the Ninth Circuit appeal, which the clerk flagged as possibly frivolous, closed in September 2023.

closed 2023 (no relief to plaintiff found)
Jan 18, 2023 · U.S. District Court, E.D. Wash. (No. 2:23-cv-00015); 9th Cir. No. 23-35403 · Spokane, WA

Court Docket

Greystar pays $2.5 million to settle Washington tenant screening-fee class action

The class alleged Greystar charged rental applicants in Washington tenant-screening fees without the advance disclosures state law requires (applications June 2016 - August 2020). Greystar denied wrongdoing and agreed to a $2.5 million settlement, which the court finalized Feb. 1, 2022.

settled $2.5M
Feb 1, 2022 · U.S. District Court, E.D. Wash. (Daley v. Greystar, 2:18-cv-00381) · Washington
