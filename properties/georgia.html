Records from Georgia

Reporting

Atlanta Magazine on the Avana on Main fire and the residents' lawsuit

In a feature on Atlanta apartment fires, Atlanta Magazine recounted the August 2020 Avana on Main fire that displaced more than 200 residents, and a now-settled residents' suit alleging an employee started it while working on rooftop HVAC units and that faulty fire alarms and expired extinguishers had gone unaddressed. The complex denied the allegations and settled with many residents; the residents' complaint (published by Law.com) named Greystar Management Services, L.P. as a defendant.

settled with many plaintiffs (per Atlanta Magazine)
Nov 26, 2025 · Atlanta Magazine · Avana on Main · Atlanta, GA

Reporting

Luxury downtown high-rise residents frustrated over building's faulty elevators

Residents of the Ascent at Peachtree high-rise said repeated elevator outages over several months forced them to climb multiple flights of stairs. FOX 5 said it made multiple attempts to reach Greystar, the property manager, without a response at publication.

published
Jun 11, 2024 · FOX 5 Atlanta · Ascent at Peachtree · Atlanta, GA

More than 50 displaced residents sue Greystar over 2020 Avana on Main apartment fire

Residents displaced by the Aug. 19, 2020 fire at Avana on Main sued GS Main Street, LLC, Greystar Management Services, L.P. and a service technician, seeking damages arising from the fire.

filed (outcome not confirmed)
Aug 19, 2021 · State Court of Fulton County, Georgia (No. 21EV004936) · Avana on Main · Atlanta, GA
