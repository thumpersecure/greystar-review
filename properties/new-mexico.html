Records from New Mexico

Reporting

Frustration reaches new level after disabled man's apartment floods twice

KOB 4 reported that a disabled Section 8 tenant's unit at Greystar-managed Copper Hills flooded in February and again in August, leaving ceiling holes, warped cabinets and ruined furniture; he said Greystar told him it could not help because he lacked renter's insurance. KOB said the leasing office was closed during business hours and it could not reach Greystar.

published
Sep 3, 2025 · KOB 4 · Copper Hills · Albuquerque, NM

Letters from Tenants

Tenant alleges roach-infested unit at move-in and repairs tied to steep rent hikes

Reviewer alleges the apartment was infested with cockroaches at move-in, upper floors had soft, patched flooring, and leaks between units were common. Reviewer alleges repairs were being made mainly in units whose rent had risen more than 30% in two years, and that online rent payment carried fees.

published
May 20, 2023 · RateTheLandlord.org · AYA ABQ · Albuquerque, NM
