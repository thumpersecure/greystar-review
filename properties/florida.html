Tenant alleges nearly three months' rent in charges for early move-out plus unused service fees

Reviewer alleges that after five months they were charged nearly three months' rent to leave early, combining a two-month termination fee and an 'insufficient notice fee', while also paying for pest control that did not stop roaches and a valet trash service no one used.

Tenant alleges management threatens residents who complain at Gateway Lakes

Reviewer alleges management responds to complaints mainly by threatening residents, failed to act on threats from neighbors, charged for valet trash service that was not provided, and advertised a gated community that was not gated. Reviewer also reports an ant infestation and a unit that differed from the model shown.

published
Dec 25, 2013 · Ripoff Report · Gateway Lakes Apartments (aka Marlin Lakes) · Sarasota, FL

Family alleges Greystar complex barred next of kin from deceased tenant's apartment

Reviewer alleges that after a long-term tenant died, management disabled the lock and refused her only next of kin access to retrieve clothing and photos, while allowing cleaners in without notice; access came only after the family obtained a court order. Reviewer further alleges the unit's walls were covered in black mold from a water leak in the unit above.

published
Mar 10, 2013 · Ripoff Report · Hawthorne Groves · Orlando, FL
