Kentucky AG sues RealPage and landlords including Greystar; Greystar later settles

Kentucky AG Russell Coleman sued RealPage and several landlords, including Greystar Real Estate Partners, alleging algorithmic price-fixing in violation of federal antitrust law and the Kentucky Consumer Protection Act. According to PYMNTS (July 21, 2026), Kentucky reached a settlement with Greystar Management Services; terms were not disclosed in public filings.

Greystar settled July 2026 (terms undisclosed)

Jul 2, 2025 · Kentucky Attorney General / U.S. District Court, E.D. Ky. · Kentucky

Kentucky Settles Antitrust Claims Against Greystar in RealPage Rent-Setting Case

Citing Law360, PYMNTS reports that Kentucky settled its antitrust claims against Greystar Management Services in AG Russell Coleman's RealPage suit, filed July 2025 in the Eastern District of Kentucky (kentucky-ag-v-realpage-greystar-2025). Terms were not disclosed. It adds that a federal judge had earlier let key claims proceed after Greystar sought dismissal, and that the complaint alleged hundreds of thousands of Kentucky renters were affected.

Greystar notifies Washington AG of data breach involving employee email account

Greystar Real Estate Partners filed a breach notice stating an unauthorized party accessed an employee email account between May 1 and May 18, 2026 and acquired files containing personal information; affected people were offered 24 months of credit monitoring. This is a mandatory disclosure, not an enforcement action; no lawsuit over this breach was found.

Housing watchdog files 114 voucher-discrimination complaints against Greystar with seven state and D.C. agencies

Housing Rights Initiative filed complaints with the Virginia OAG, California CRD, Maryland Commission on Civil Rights, Hawai'i Civil Rights Commission, Michigan DCR, New Jersey DCR and D.C. Office of Human Rights alleging 114 violations of source-of-income laws. The complaints allege, based on undercover testing, that Greystar properties rejected Housing Choice Voucher holders, imposed unlawful conditions, and refused to count voucher assistance toward income requirements.

Greystar California agrees to revise tenant screening statewide after fair housing complaint

CRD announced a settlement with Greystar California, Inc. after a complaint alleging an applicant at a Hollywood apartment complex was denied based on criminal history without an opportunity to explain. Greystar agreed to review and revise screening policies statewide, notify staff of California's criminal-history protections, train specified Southern California staff, and pay the complainant $10,000.

settled $10,000 plus policy changes
Jan 13, 2026 · California Civil Rights Department · Los Angeles, CA
