Greystar, others settle RealPage rental pricing lawsuit

Trade-press coverage of the RealPage MDL class settlements in the Middle District of Tennessee (realpage-mdl-greystar-50m-class-settlement-2025). It adds the full picture: 26 class settlements worth $141.8 million, with Greystar's $50 million the largest and Simpson, Avenue5, Bell, Bozzuto, Pinnacle and Winn also settling. Settlers agreed to stop sharing nonpublic data with RealPage's rent-setting algorithm, with no admission of wrongdoing.
