Greystar pays $2.5 million to settle Washington tenant screening-fee class action

The class alleged Greystar charged rental applicants in Washington tenant-screening fees without the advance disclosures state law requires (applications June 2016 - August 2020). Greystar denied wrongdoing and agreed to a $2.5 million settlement, which received final approval Feb. 1, 2022.

settled $2.5M

Feb 1, 2022 · U.S. District Court, E.D. Wash. (Daley v. Greystar, 2:18-cv-00381) · Washington
