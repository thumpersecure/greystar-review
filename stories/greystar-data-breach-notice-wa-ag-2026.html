Greystar notifies Washington AG of data breach involving employee email account

Greystar Real Estate Partners filed a breach notice stating an unauthorized party accessed an employee email account between May 1 and May 18, 2026 and acquired files containing personal information; affected people were offered 24 months of credit monitoring. This is a mandatory disclosure, not an enforcement action; no lawsuit over this breach was found.

Greystar pays $2.5 million to settle Washington tenant screening-fee class action

The class alleged Greystar charged rental applicants in Washington tenant-screening fees without the advance disclosures state law requires (applications June 2016 - August 2020). Greystar denied wrongdoing and agreed to a $2.5 million settlement, which the court finalized Feb. 1, 2022.

Greystar California agrees to revise tenant screening statewide after fair housing complaint

CRD announced a settlement with Greystar California, Inc. after a complaint alleging an applicant at a Hollywood apartment complex was denied based on criminal history without an opportunity to explain. Greystar agreed to review and revise screening policies statewide, notify staff of California's criminal-history protections, train specified Southern California staff, and pay the complainant $10,000.
