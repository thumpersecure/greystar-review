Greystar agrees to $50 million settlement in renters' RealPage price-fixing class action

In the consolidated private antitrust MDL alleging landlords used RealPage software to align rents, Greystar agreed to pay $50 million as part of more than $141 million in proposed settlements filed with 25+ landlords, and to stop sharing nonpublic data with RealPage. Greystar announced an agreement in principle on Aug. 8, 2025, stating it admitted no wrongdoing.
