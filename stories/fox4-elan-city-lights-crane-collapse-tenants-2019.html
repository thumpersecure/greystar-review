Dallas jury returns $860 million verdict against Greystar over fatal 2019 crane collapse

A Dallas County jury awarded about $860 million to the parents of a resident killed when a tower crane at a Greystar development collapsed onto the neighboring Elan City Lights apartments during a June 2019 storm. Plaintiffs argued Greystar let the crane's inspection lapse and was responsible for the crane not being left in weathervane mode; the jury found crane owner Bigge not liable.
