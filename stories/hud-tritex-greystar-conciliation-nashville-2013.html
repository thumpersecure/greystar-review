HUD conciliation agreement with Greystar over alleged harassment of Hispanic tenants in Nashville

A HUD Secretary-initiated Fair Housing Act complaint filed Feb. 10, 2012 alleged TriTex Real Estate Advisors and Greystar Management Services subjected Hispanic residents of the 233-unit Clairmont Apartments to different terms, intimidation and harassment based on national origin. Respondents denied discrimination and entered a conciliation agreement without any finding of liability.

2013 · U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (FHEO Case No. 04-12-0360-8) · Clairmont Apartments · Nashville, TN
