Class action accuses Greystar and nine other managers of hiding Facebook housing ads from older renters

Housing Rights Initiative and an older D.C. resident sued 10 property managers, including Greystar, alleging they targeted Facebook apartment ads to exclude users over 50; they had earlier filed age-bias charges with D.C. and Montgomery County human rights offices (Sept. 2019). The district case closed in July 2021 and the Fourth Circuit affirmed dismissal on standing grounds in March 2025.

dismissed (standing); affirmed on appeal 2025

Jul 1, 2020 · U.S. District Court, D. Md. (Opiotennione v. Bozzuto Management Co., 8:20-cv-01956) · Washington, DC

Greystar agrees to $50 million settlement in renters' RealPage price-fixing class action

In the consolidated private antitrust MDL alleging landlords used RealPage software to align rents, Greystar agreed to pay $50 million as part of more than $141 million in proposed settlements filed with 25+ landlords, and to stop sharing nonpublic data with RealPage. Greystar announced an agreement in principle on Aug. 8, 2025, stating it admitted no wrongdoing.
