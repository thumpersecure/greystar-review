Colorado settles hidden fees lawsuit against Greystar in the latest case resolved with landlord giant

Denver Post coverage of the FTC/Colorado $24 million hidden-fees settlement (ftc-colorado-greystar-24m-settlement-2025). It adds that the suit described two Denver complexes where fees allegedly pushed costs above advertised rent, and that Greystar operates more than 45,000 units in Colorado. It also quotes Greystar saying the deal 'enhances transparency' and notes Colorado's new rent-fee law takes effect Jan. 1.

settled $24M

Dec 2, 2025 · The Denver Post · Colorado

Attorney General Phil Weiser, FTC announce $24M settlement with Greystar for misleading renters about costs, saddling tenants with hidden fees

Colorado's announcement of the FTC/Colorado hidden-fees settlement already recorded from the FTC side (ftc-colorado-greystar-24m-settlement-2025). This source adds the split: Colorado receives $1 million for costs, enforcement and consumer education, while the FTC receives $23 million for nationwide relief. Housing watchdog files 114 voucher-discrimination complaints against Greystar with seven state and D.C. agencies

Housing Rights Initiative filed complaints with the Virginia OAG, California CRD, Maryland Commission on Civil Rights, Hawai'i Civil Rights Commission, Michigan DCR, New Jersey DCR and D.C. Office of Human Rights alleging 114 violations of source-of-income laws. The complaints allege, based on undercover testing, that Greystar properties rejected Housing Choice Voucher holders, imposed unlawful conditions, and refused to count voucher assistance toward income requirements.
