Greystar Lawsuits and Class Actions

This docket lists civil lawsuits and class actions that name Greystar or its affiliates, with court, case number where available, and current status. Filed complaints contain allegations that have not been proven; where a case settled, we note whether Greystar admitted wrongdoing. Each entry links to the court record or the reporting it is based on.

2026

Kentucky Settles Antitrust Claims Against Greystar in RealPage Rent-Setting Case

Citing Law360, PYMNTS reports that Kentucky settled its antitrust claims against Greystar Management Services in AG Russell Coleman's RealPage suit, filed July 2025 in the Eastern District of Kentucky (kentucky-ag-v-realpage-greystar-2025). Terms were not disclosed. It adds that a federal judge had earlier let key claims proceed after Greystar sought dismissal, and that the complaint alleged hundreds of thousands of Kentucky renters were affected.

settled (terms undisclosed)
Jul 21, 2026 · PYMNTS.com · Kentucky

Greystar Settles Out Of Kentucky's RealPage Antitrust Case

Law360 reports that Kentucky settled its federal antitrust claims against Greystar Management Services, which the state alleged used RealPage revenue-management software to fix rents. This is the source of the July 2026 Greystar settlement noted on kentucky-ag-v-realpage-greystar-2025.

settled (terms undisclosed)
Jul 20, 2026 · Law360 Real Estate Authority · Kentucky

Judge OKs Greystar Deal In DOJ's RealPage Price-Fixing Suit

According to Law360, a North Carolina federal judge gave final sign-off on March 2, 2026 to the Justice Department's antitrust settlement with Greystar Management Services in the federal RealPage rent price-fixing case. This finalizes the consent decree proposed in August 2025 (doj-greystar-realpage-consent-decree-2025).

settled (consent decree finalized by court)
Mar 2, 2026 · Law360 · North Carolina
2025

Greystar, others settle RealPage rental pricing lawsuit

Trade-press coverage of the RealPage MDL class settlements in the Middle District of Tennessee (realpage-mdl-greystar-50m-class-settlement-2025). It adds the full picture: 26 class settlements worth $141.8 million, with Greystar's $50 million the largest and Simpson, Avenue5, Bell, Bozzuto, Pinnacle and Winn also settling. Settlers agreed to stop sharing nonpublic data with RealPage's rent-setting algorithm, with no admission of wrongdoing.

settled $50M (class settlement pending before the court)
Oct 8, 2025 · Multifamily Dive · Nashville, TN

Greystar agrees to $50 million settlement in RealPage rental pricing lawsuit

Reuters report on Greystar's $50 million settlement of the renter class action in the RealPage MDL (realpage-mdl-greystar-50m-class-settlement-2025). Renters alleged Greystar and other landlords shared nonpublic data with RealPage to push rents above competitive levels. Greystar's deal was the largest in a group of preliminary class settlements totaling about $141 million.

settled $50M (class settlement pending before the court)
Oct 2, 2025 · Reuters · Nashville, TN

Greystar agrees to $50 million settlement in renters' RealPage price-fixing class action

In the consolidated private antitrust MDL alleging landlords used RealPage software to align rents, Greystar agreed to pay $50 million as part of more than $141 million in proposed settlements filed with 25+ landlords, and to stop sharing nonpublic data with RealPage. Greystar announced an agreement in principle on Aug. 8, 2025, stating it admitted no wrongdoing.

settled $50M (proposed class settlement)
Oct 1, 2025 · U.S. District Court, M.D. Tenn. (In re RealPage, Inc., Rental Software Antitrust Litigation (No. II), 3:23-md-03071) · Nashville, TN

Littleton tenant sues Greystar alleging eviction in retaliation for silica dust complaints

A former tenant sued Greystar Management Services and a concrete contractor, alleging he was exposed to silica dust from concrete work, reported it to Greystar and OSHA (which fined the contractor more than $58,000), and was then evicted for allegedly 'photographing the vendors without consent.' Greystar denies most allegations and says it had no control over the contractor's work.

pending (as reported)

Class action alleges Greystar charged California tenants undisclosed junk fees

The proposed class action alleges Greystar 'drip priced' California rentals by charging mandatory fees for pest control, trash and utility administration not included in advertised rent, in violation of the UCL, False Advertising Law, CLRA and California's Honest Pricing Law.

pending
Apr 29, 2025 · U.S. District Court, S.D. Cal. (Wu et al. v. Greystar Real Estate Partners, LLC, 3:25-cv-01090) · California

Washington class action challenges Greystar lease buy-out and reletting charges

Filed on behalf of tenants who moved out early and were charged, or had deposits applied to, buy-out fees, reletting charges and/or future rent. Greystar removed the case to federal court, which remanded it to King County Superior Court.

pending (trial set Feb 2027 per counsel)

2024

Colorado tenant class action accuses Greystar of charging inflated junk fees

The class complaint alleges Greystar forced thousands of Colorado tenants to pay inflated, late-disclosed 'junk fees' on top of advertised rent that provided no benefit beyond ordinary landlord costs, and seeks to stop the practice and recover damages.

filed (outcome not confirmed)
Jan 5, 2024 · District Court, Denver County, Colorado (Collins v. GREP Southwest, LLC d/b/a Greystar) · Denver, CO
2023

Boston tenants' class action alleges Greystar entity charged unlawful eviction legal fees

Tenants of The Eddy in East Boston sued Greystar affiliate GREP Atlantic and the building owner, alleging 'eviction/legal recovery' fees were charged without a court judgment, in violation of the Massachusetts Consumer Protection Act. The court dismissed one claim but allowed two to proceed, and allowed some Greystar counterclaims.

pending
Nov 29, 2023 · Boston Globe (U.S. District Court, D. Mass.) · The Eddy · Boston, MA

Dallas jury returns $860 million verdict against Greystar over fatal 2019 crane collapse

A Dallas County jury awarded about $860 million to the parents of a resident killed when a tower crane at a Greystar development collapsed onto the neighboring Elan City Lights apartments during a June 2019 storm. Plaintiffs argued Greystar let the crane's inspection lapse and was responsible for the crane not being left in weathervane mode; the jury found crane owner Bigge not liable.

jury verdict $860M (post-trial status not confirmed)
Apr 26, 2023 · The Real Deal (Dallas County district court verdict) · Elan City Lights · Dallas, TX

Gamez v. Greystar: pro se civil rights suit naming Greystar and CEO Bob Faith

A self-represented tenant filed a civil rights suit against Greystar Real Estate Partners, Bob Faith (individually and in his official capacity), a property-owning LLC, Spokane County, a law firm and others. The district case closed in May 2023; the Ninth Circuit appeal, which the clerk flagged as possibly frivolous, closed in September 2023.

closed 2023 (no relief to plaintiff found)
Jan 18, 2023 · U.S. District Court, E.D. Wash. (No. 2:23-cv-00015); 9th Cir. No. 23-35403 · Spokane, WA
2022

Greystar pays $4.665 million to settle North Carolina eviction-fee class action

Tenants alleged Greystar unlawfully charged North Carolina residents filing fees, sheriff service fees and attorneys' fees in eviction actions and threatened such fees in collection letters (2014-2018), in violation of state rental and debt-collection law. Greystar agreed to a $4,665,000 class settlement without admitting wrongdoing (final fairness hearing July 22, 2022).

settled $4.665M
Jul 22, 2022 · U.S. District Court, M.D.N.C. (Wallace v. Greystar Real Estate Partners, LLC, 1:18-cv-00501) · North Carolina

Greystar pays $2.5 million to settle Washington tenant screening-fee class action

The class alleged Greystar charged rental applicants in Washington tenant-screening fees without the advance disclosures state law requires (applications June 2016 - August 2020). Greystar denied wrongdoing and agreed to a $2.5 million settlement, which the court finalized Feb. 1, 2022.

settled $2.5M
Feb 1, 2022 · U.S. District Court, E.D. Wash. (Daley v. Greystar, 2:18-cv-00381) · Washington

Class action accuses Greystar and nine other managers of hiding Facebook housing ads from older renters

Housing Rights Initiative and an older D.C. resident sued 10 property managers, including Greystar, alleging they targeted Facebook apartment ads to exclude users over 50; they had earlier filed age-bias charges with D.C. and Montgomery County human rights offices (Sept. 2019). The district case closed in July 2021 and the Fourth Circuit affirmed dismissal on standing grounds in March 2025.

Hall v. Greystar Management Services: Fair Housing Act retaliation and eviction-property suit

A former tenant sued Greystar Management Services, a landscaping contractor and a sheriff's lieutenant, alleging Fair Housing Act retaliation, conversion and constitutional claims arising from the alleged deprivation of her personal property during a December 2011 eviction. The case went to the Fourth Circuit (No. 14-2145) and back; the district court issued further rulings in 2016 and the docket closed in February 2017 without a judgment for the plaintiff.

closed 2017 (plaintiff did not prevail)
Apr 12, 2016 · U.S. District Court, D. Md. (No. 1:13-cv-03615), 179 F. Supp. 3d 534 · Baltimore County, MD
