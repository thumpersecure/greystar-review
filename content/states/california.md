The most recent California regulator action in the record is a January 2026 settlement: the California Civil Rights Department (CRD) announced that Greystar California, Inc. [agreed to revise tenant screening statewide]({{story:ca-crd-greystar-criminal-history-screening-2026}}) and pay a complainant $10,000. The record describes a fair housing complaint alleging an applicant at a Hollywood apartment complex was denied based on criminal history without an opportunity to explain. Greystar also agreed to notify staff of California's criminal-history protections and train specified Southern California staff. [The CRD announcement]({{src:ca-crd-greystar-criminal-history-screening-2026}}) is the primary source.

## Lawsuits and class actions

Two federal class actions over fees appear in the record.

- **Wu et al. v. Greystar Real Estate Partners, LLC** (S.D. Cal., 3:25-cv-01090) is a [proposed class action filed in April 2025]({{story:wu-v-greystar-california-junk-fees-2025}}). It alleges Greystar "drip priced" California rentals by charging mandatory fees for pest control, trash and utility administration that were not included in advertised rent, citing the UCL, False Advertising Law, CLRA and California's Honest Pricing Law. Status in the record: pending.
- **Zeff v. Greystar Real Estate Partners, LLC** (N.D. Cal., 3:20-cv-07122) was [filed in October 2020]({{story:zeff-v-greystar-late-fees-2020}}). The complaint alleges $100 late fees untethered to actual damages, late fees that "stacked" month to month, unauthorized $3.95 utility-administration fees, and mishandled security-deposit deductions. The outcome is not confirmed in the record.

These are allegations in complaints, not findings. The same fee theme led to a federal-state settlement elsewhere; see the [Colorado report]({{state:colorado}}) for the FTC and Colorado case.

## Multistate settlements and regulator complaints

California is part of the nine-state $7 million settlement over alleged algorithmic rent pricing announced by Attorney General Rob Bonta ([record]({{story:nine-state-ag-greystar-7m-settlement-2025}})). The [Long Beach Post]({{story:lbpost-greystar-7m-multistate-settlement-2025}}) reported the roughly $7 million settlement in November 2025. [Pleasanton Weekly]({{story:greystar-reaches-7m-settlement-in-antitrust-lawsuit-over-rent-increase-2025}}) added a Greystar spokesperson statement and listed Greystar-managed properties in Mountain View and on the Peninsula, noting that Bonta's office said it could not say whether Santa Clara or San Mateo County properties were affected. A settlement is a resolution of the claims, not an admission by itself.

In July 2026, Housing Rights Initiative [filed 114 voucher-discrimination complaints]({{story:hri-multistate-voucher-discrimination-complaints-2026}}) with seven state and D.C. agencies, including the California CRD. The complaints allege, based on undercover testing, that Greystar properties rejected Housing Choice Voucher holders. The record lists them as pending allegations. Other agencies named include Hawai'i; see the [Hawaii report]({{state:hawaii}}) and the [District of Columbia report]({{state:district-of-columbia}}).

## Reporting on conditions and displacement

- **Berkeley balcony collapse.** [KTVU reported]({{story:ktvu-berkeley-balcony-collapse-settlement-2017}}) in November 2017 that victims of the June 2015 Library Gardens collapse, which killed six young people and injured seven, reached a confidential settlement with owner BlackRock and property manager Greystar, and that the parties agreed to adopt regular balcony-inspection policies.
- **San Jose.** [San Jose Inside reported]({{story:sanjoseinside-reserve-mass-eviction-2016}}) in June 2016 that the City Council voted 7-3 to deny tenant appeals and allow Greystar to demolish the 216-unit rent-controlled Reserve for a 640-unit project, displacing about 670 people.
- **Los Angeles.** [2UrbanGirls reported]({{story:2urbangirls-sb-main-dtla-uninhabitable-2022}}) that the City of Los Angeles deemed the Greystar-managed SB Main building uninhabitable on Sept. 1, 2022 and ordered repairs by Oct. 12.

## Forty55 Lofts

A [Proposition 65 60-day notice]({{story:forty55-lofts-prop65-notice-2021}}) (AG No. 2021-02813) filed by a private noticing party alleges formaldehyde exposure from building materials at Greystar-managed Forty55 Lofts. It is an allegation, and the record does not show an outcome. A [2024 Change.org petition]({{story:change-org-forty55-lofts-air-quality-petition}}) by a resident makes related claims about indoor air quality and had 108 signatures when reviewed.

## Tenant complaints (unverified)

The record includes single-person tenant accounts from Ripoff Report and RateTheLandlord. They are unverified and describe individual experiences, not established facts. Examples include allegations of [an unrepaired leak and a 30% rent increase in Culver City]({{story:ratethelandlord-4569-culver-city}}), [slow repairs and termites near La Jolla]({{story:ratethelandlord-4666-axiom-la-jolla}}), [late-fee threats in San Diego]({{story:ratethelandlord-4293-san-diego}}), [an eviction attempt in Montclair]({{story:ratethelandlord-4052-montclair}}), [a returned small-claims check]({{story:ripoffreport-1323321-bay-street-judgment-check}}) and [appliance and entry complaints in Tustin]({{story:ripoffreport-1471776-coventry-court-tustin}}). Older accounts concern [security gates]({{story:ripoffreport-960286-oceano-security}}), [a billing dispute]({{story:ripoffreport-957393-oceano-warner-woodland-hills}}) and [mid-lease utility charges]({{story:ripoffreport-181649-riverstone-brookstone-covina}}).
