The Chairman: Bob Faith

Bob Faith is the founder and chief executive of Greystar. This page collects public records and reporting that name him, including a 2020 appointment to a White House economic advisory group and court filings in which he was named. Inclusion here does not imply any personal wrongdoing; each entry states its source and outcome.

Court Docket · The Chairman

Gamez v. Greystar: pro se civil rights suit naming Greystar and CEO Bob Faith

A self-represented tenant filed a civil rights suit against Greystar Real Estate Partners, Bob Faith (individually and in his official capacity), a property-owning LLC, Spokane County, a law firm and others. The district case closed in May 2023; the Ninth Circuit appeal, which the clerk flagged as possibly frivolous, closed in September 2023.

Reporting · The Chairman

When Private Equity Becomes Your Landlord

ProPublica's analysis of private-equity-backed apartment buyers featured tenants of San Francisco's Olume, who described soaring rents, trash in hallways and reduced security after Greystar took over in 2017 through a Freddie Mac-financed deal. The story profiles founder Bob Faith as a pioneer of connecting institutional investors with apartment buying; Greystar said resident satisfaction was 'very important.'

published
Feb 7, 2022 · ProPublica · Olume (San Francisco) · Nationwide

Tenant alleges repeated appliance failures, unauthorized entry and ignored theft reports at Tustin complex

Reviewer alleges appliances failed repeatedly after a management change in 2013, that staff entered her apartment without 24-hour notice, and that thefts she reported to management, a regional supervisor, and Greystar's CEO were never investigated. Reviewer also alleges she had to buy her own dryer after management refused to replace a failing unit.
